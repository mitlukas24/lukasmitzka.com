Why tailored

A standard sequence cannot know what matters first.

A course book can provide structure. It cannot decide whether your priority is a meeting, an exam, precise writing or everyday life in Vienna.

Tailored does not mean improvised. Grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation and language skills remain systematic. Their order and emphasis follow what you need German to do.

The approach

Personal in focus. Systematic in structure.

The programme is planned through five connected decisions. Together, they define what receives attention, in which order and in which form.

  1. 01

    Goal

    What should German make possible next?

    The immediate purpose sets the priorities: a meeting, life in Vienna, an exam or more precise expression.

  2. 02

    Current ability

    What already works, and where does communication still break down?

    A level label is only a reference. Usable strengths and recurring barriers define the real starting point.

  3. 03

    Real situations

    Where, with whom and under what pressure does German have to work?

    Real situations shape the register, vocabulary and practice. A presentation, formal email and administrative appointment demand different language.

  4. 04

    Suitable format

    Which rhythm and setting are effective and realistic?

    Time, intensity and independent work determine the rhythm. Training may be ongoing or intensive, in Vienna or online.

  5. 05

    Review

    What has improved, and what deserves attention next?

    Progress and recurring difficulties are reviewed. The structure stays clear while priorities change when needed.

The result is not a fixed package imposed in advance, but a coherent programme with a clear direction.

Training

Three formats. One planning principle.

01

Private Training

For sustained development centred on one person’s real use of German.

Systematic language development is connected to professional, academic and everyday situations. Priorities are developed, tested in real use and refined over time.

Also available for two people with closely aligned goals.

02

Intensive Programmes

For a defined period in which concentrated progress matters.

Designed for a move, new role, examination, project or other time-sensitive transition. The work is concentrated and requires sufficient time during and between sessions.

Scope is agreed after the goal, level and realistic workload are clear.

03

Training for Organisations

For employees who need German in specific professional situations.

Planned around actual roles, communication tasks and workplace contexts rather than a generic business-language syllabus.

Possible focuses include meetings, presentations, written communication, onboarding and practical integration in Vienna.

About

Serious preparation, clear explanations and language that has to work outside the lesson.

I am a German educator based in Vienna with a Bachelor of Education and completed specialist training in German as a foreign and second language. I have taught adults, international professionals, intensive-course participants and company clients in individual and group settings.

My work combines structured language teaching with the situations in which German is actually needed. Grammar is explained precisely, vocabulary is developed in context and recurring difficulties are addressed directly.

The aim is simple: focused work that is understandable in the lesson and useful outside it.
