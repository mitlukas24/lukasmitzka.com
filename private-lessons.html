Overview

Designed around your needs.

A tailored format begins with your goals, current level, time frame and the situations where you actually need German.

Possible focus areas

  • A1 to C1 German
  • Speaking confidence and active language use
  • Grammar clarity without isolated drilling
  • Writing, pronunciation and vocabulary
  • Professional or everyday communication
  • Exam preparation where relevant

1. Initial orientation

Clarify goals, level, preferred format and realistic next steps.

2. Tailored planning

Select materials, topics and practice formats that fit your context.

3. Active progress

Work with clear feedback and regular active language use.
