German for professionals in Vienna

For professionals, German is not only about vocabulary. It is about participation, precision and presence.

The right words change how you participate.

Even with excellent English, professional life in Austria can create situations where German matters: meetings, informal conversations, emails, networking, client communication and workplace culture.

Professional German is not about sounding perfect. It is about communicating with control.
