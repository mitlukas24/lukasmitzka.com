About Lukas

Vienna-based German teacher for adults, internationals, professionals, small groups and educational contexts.

Lukas Mitzka, BEd

Lukas Mitzka, BEd, is a Vienna-based German teacher with experience in German as a Foreign and Second Language, working with adults, young learners, international students, small groups and professional clients.

His teaching is shaped by clear structure, careful preparation and a strong focus on active language use.
