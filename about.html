Profile

How I teach

Calm, structured and precise.

I do not believe in overwhelming learners with random exercises. Good language training needs clear goals, useful repetition, active speaking and feedback that learners can actually understand and use.

clear structure active communication precise correction realistic goals useful repetition language in context respect for different learning biographies

Language learning

Understanding language learning from the inside

Learning, repeating and building confidence

My own interest in languages helps me understand what it means to learn, forget, repeat, hesitate, compare structures and build confidence in another language.

Explaining clearly

This experience helps me explain grammar clearly, compare structures when useful and understand the uncertainty many learners feel when speaking.

International learners

Working with different learning biographies

I work with learners from different linguistic, cultural and professional backgrounds. Some need German for everyday life in Vienna. Others need it for work, university, interviews, exams or long-term integration.

There is no single standard path. Good training needs to understand where learners start, what they need and what kind of progress is realistic.

Why this work matters to me

Motivation

German can make everyday and professional life less stressful.

Language can make people more independent. It can make professional life easier, everyday situations less stressful and participation in a new environment more possible.

My aim is to make German clearer, more manageable and more useful for the situations learners actually face.
